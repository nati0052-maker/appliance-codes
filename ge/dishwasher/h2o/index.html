GE Dishwasher H2O Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 10 minutesAlso shown as H20
Short answer: H2O means the dishwasher is getting too little water, or none, during the fill. The fill runs on a timer, so low pressure or a partly closed supply valve leaves the tub short. It shows up most often on a brand new install, right after plumbing work, or after someone turned the water off under the sink.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Open the hot water valve all the way. Find the valve under the sink nearest the dishwasher and turn it fully open. A valve left half open after an install or repair is the most common cause.
  2. Check the supply hose for kinks. Look along the line under the sink and, if you can, behind the kick plate at the bottom front of the dishwasher. A pinched line cuts the flow.
  3. Check that the dishwasher is level. If it tilts forward or back, the water collects at one end and the level can read low. Adjust the feet until the door sits evenly.
  4. Clear the code. Select a different cycle option, then set it back to the one you want. If that does not clear it, switch off power at the breaker for a minute or two, then try again.

Run the kitchen tap before starting the cycle. If the flow is weak there too, the issue is the home's water supply rather than the dishwasher.

When to call a technician

If the valve is fully open, the water pressure is good, the dishwasher is level, and H2O keeps returning, GE recommends a service call. GE also says that if H2O appears only during the 1-Hour Wash cycle, you should schedule service, but you can keep using other cycles until then.
