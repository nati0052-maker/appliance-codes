GE Dishwasher C3 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually needs a technicianAbout 5 minutes to try a resetMeans no drain
Short answer: C3 means the dishwasher will not drain. GE's fix is a power reset: switch the dishwasher off at the circuit breaker for about 30 seconds, then turn it back on. If C3 returns, GE recommends service. Before you call, a few quick checks of the drain path can rule out a simple clog.

Try it yourself first: four checks, in order

  1. Reset the power. Turn off the circuit breaker for the dishwasher, wait about 30 seconds, and turn it back on. This is the step GE lists for C3, and it clears the code if the control simply needs a restart.
  2. Run the garbage disposer. If the dishwasher drains into a disposer, run it with cold water for a few seconds to clear food that can back up the drain.
  3. Clean the air gap and check the hose. If there is a small cap on the sink deck next to the faucet, lift it off and clear any debris inside. Then follow the drain hose under the sink and straighten any kinks. These are the checks GE gives for its general failure to drain code.
  4. Run a short cycle. Start a rinse or short cycle and listen for the drain pump. If the tub empties and C3 does not come back, you are done.

Standing water in the tub is normal while this code is active. You can soak it up with a towel or sponge before working inside. If a slow drain was the warning sign before this happened, the C1 page has a diagram of the air gap and drain hose.

When to call a technician

If C3 comes back after the power reset and the drain checks, GE recommends contacting service. A drain pump that will not run or is blocked inside needs the dishwasher pulled out and opened up, which is a technician job. Turn off the power at the breaker before anyone works on it.
