LG Dryer d80 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 30 minutesAlso shown as d90, d95
Short answer: d80, d90 and d95 mean the dryer's exhaust airflow is restricted. The higher the number, the worse the blockage: LG describes d90 as 90% blocked and d95 as 95% blocked. The usual causes are lint in the filter or duct, a crushed hose behind the dryer, or a stuck outside vent flap. LG advises not using the dryer until the vent is cleared.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Clean the lint filter. Remove all lint and wash the filter with water if it looks clogged. Some LG models have an inner and outer filter; clean both.
  2. Feel the airflow outside. Start the dryer and go to the outside vent. You should feel a strong stream of air and the flap should open fully. Weak or no airflow confirms a restriction in the path.
  3. Check the hose behind the dryer. Unplug the dryer first (and turn off the gas on a gas model). Pull it out and look for a hose that is crushed, dented, kinked or torn. Disconnect it, clear any lint, and reconnect. LG recommends rigid or semi-rigid duct, run straight or in a gentle S curve.
  4. Clear the outside vent hood. Remove lint, nests or debris around the hood. If the flap is damaged or will not open fully when the dryer runs, replace the vent cover.
  5. Test again. Plug the dryer back in and run an Air Dry cycle. If the code clears, keep cleaning the lint filter after every load.

On gas models, LG's code list notes that d80 can also show up when the gas supply is low. Make sure the gas valve behind the dryer is fully open.

When to call a technician

If the filter, hose and outside hood are all clear and the code returns, the blockage is probably inside the wall duct. Long duct runs and many 90 degree bends make this more likely. LG recommends a professional duct cleaning, and a yearly vent cleaning in general. If the vent is confirmed clear and the code still appears, request LG service.
