LG Washer LE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Some fixes need a technician5 to 30 minutesAlso shown as LE1
Short answer: LE means the drum cannot turn because the motor is locked. LG lists an overloaded washer, running cycles back to back without a break, and a coin or other object caught between the drum and the tub as the usual causes.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Reset the washer. Unplug it and wait 5 minutes. LG also suggests pressing and holding Power for a few seconds while unplugged to fully discharge the control.
  2. Let it rest. If the washer has run several heavy cycles in a row, the motor may be overloaded. LG recommends letting it rest for about 30 minutes before restarting.
  3. Reduce the load. Take some laundry out. An overstuffed drum can stop the motor from turning.
  4. Look for trapped objects. With the drum empty, turn it by hand and listen for scraping. On top loaders, check the gap between the wash plate and the drum for coins, pins, or clips.

Restart a short cycle with a normal-sized load.

When to call a technician

If the drum is empty, turns freely by hand, and LE still appears, the cause is likely the motor, its sensor, or the control. These need LG service or a technician.
