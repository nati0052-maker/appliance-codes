LG Washer UE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 10 minutesAlso shown as uE
Short answer: UE means the washer could not balance the load, even after tumbling it to spread it out. LG lists uneven laundry, a load that is too large or too small, mixed heavy and light items, and a washer that is not level as the causes.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Pause and spread the laundry. Open the door, separate tangled items, and spread them evenly around the drum, then restart.
  2. Fix the load size. LG says the washer balances best at about two thirds full. A tiny load with one heavy item is a common trigger; add a few similar items.
  3. Separate heavy and light items. Jeans with t-shirts, or one blanket or comforter alone, tend to bunch on one side. Wash bulky items on the cycle meant for them.
  4. Level the washer. If it thumps or rocks during spin, adjust the feet until it sits flat on the floor.

If UE appears at the end of a cycle, the clothes are still too wet: rearrange them and run Spin Only.

When to call a technician

If the washer is level and loads are sensible but UE keeps appearing, LG says the unit may require service.
