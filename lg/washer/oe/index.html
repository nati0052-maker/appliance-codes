LG Washer OE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 20 to 30 minutesAlso shown as E03, E21
Short answer: OE means the washer cannot drain the water from the wash cycle. LG lists a kinked, clogged, or frozen drain hose and a clogged drain pump filter as the main causes. On top loaders the code appears after the washer has been unable to drain for about 13 minutes.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Check the drain hose behind the washer. Pull the washer out and make sure the hose is not bent, crushed against the wall, or clogged. In cold weather, check it is not frozen; a warm wet cloth wrapped around it helps it thaw.
  2. Check the hose height. A drain hose installed too high can stop the washer from pumping the water out. Follow the height in your installation guide.
  3. Listen to the pump. Run a Spin Only cycle. If you hear the pump humming but the water does not go down, the filter is likely blocked.
  4. Clean the drain pump filter (front loaders). Put a shallow pan under the small residual drain hose at the bottom front, drain the water, then twist out the filter and remove lint and debris. The water may be hot.
  5. Clear excess suds. Too much detergent creates air pockets the pump cannot remove. Use HE detergent in the right amount and run a Tub Clean cycle.

Run a Spin Only cycle to confirm the water drains and the code clears.

When to call a technician

If the hose and filter are clear and OE comes back, LG says the unit may need repair. The usual culprit is a failed drain pump.
