LG Dishwasher LE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually needs a technicianAbout 5 minutes to try a resetWash motor error
Short answer: LE means the dishwasher has detected a problem with its wash motor, the motor that drives the water through the spray arms. LG says it may be the motor itself or the wiring between the motor and the control board. A power reset is worth one try. If LE comes back, LG's advice is to request service.

Try a reset first: four steps

  1. Turn the dishwasher off. Press the power button so the display goes dark.
  2. Switch off the circuit breaker for 10 seconds. Find the breaker for the dishwasher in your home's electrical panel. Turn it off, wait at least 10 seconds, then turn it back on. If your dishwasher plugs into an outlet under the sink, unplugging it for the same time works the same way.
  3. Start another cycle. Power the dishwasher on and run a cycle. Stay nearby for the first few minutes and watch the display.
  4. Write down the details if LE returns. Note the model and serial number (on a label around the edge of the door or tub opening), when the code appeared and anything you heard, such as humming or no wash sound at all. This saves time when you book service.

Do not keep restarting the dishwasher over and over to clear the code. If the reset does not hold, the fault needs to be diagnosed.

When to call a technician

If LE comes back after the reset. LG lists this code as one that needs inspection by a service technician. Checking the motor and its wiring means removing the lower panels with the power isolated, so leave it to LG service or a qualified appliance technician. If the dishwasher is under warranty, contact LG before anyone opens it.
