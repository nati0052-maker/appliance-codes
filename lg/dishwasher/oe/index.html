LG Dishwasher OE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 20 minutes
Short answer: OE means the dishwasher detected that the water is not fully draining from the tub. LG says it most often comes from food clogging the filters or an installation problem with the drain hose.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Remove food debris. Take out any large food scraps or items in the bottom of the tub.
  2. Clean the filters. Twist out the filter assembly, rinse the mesh and the stainless filter, and refit it until it clicks. LG suggests cleaning the filter every week or two.
  3. Check the drain hose under the sink. Make sure it is not bent, crushed by stored items, or frozen.
  4. Check the garbage disposal. On a new disposal, the knockout plug where the dishwasher hose connects must be removed.
  5. Note when it started. If OE appeared on the very first cycle, LG says it points to installation: the drain hose may be kinked, too high, or too long. The installer should correct it.

Run a short cycle to confirm the water drains.

When to call a technician

If the filters and hose are clear and OE continues, the drain pump may have failed. Unplug the dishwasher and request service.
