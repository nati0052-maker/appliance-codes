Samsung Dishwasher 4C Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 10 minutesAlso shown as 4E
Short answer: 4C (or 4E) means the dishwasher is not receiving enough water during the fill. Samsung lists a closed or partly closed supply valve, a kinked, frozen, or clogged inlet line, and low water pressure as the usual causes.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Open the supply valve fully. The valve is usually under the sink. Turn it counterclockwise to open. After installation work it is often left half closed.
  2. Check for a water outage. Run the sink tap. If there is little or no water there, the problem is the home supply.
  3. Check the inlet hose. Look under the sink for kinks or a frozen line. Samsung also warns that FloodSafe-type hoses are not compatible with its dishwashers.
  4. Check pressure and temperature. Samsung specifies 20 to 120 psi water pressure and supply water no hotter than 176°F (80°C).

Turn the dishwasher off and on, then start a short cycle.

When to call a technician

If the supply is fine and 4C continues, the water inlet valve, its filter, or the flow meter may be blocked or faulty. These sit behind the dishwasher's lower panel, so Samsung recommends service.
