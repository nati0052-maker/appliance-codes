Samsung Dishwasher LC Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Some fixes need a technicianAllow drying timeAlso shown as LE
Short answer: LC (or LE) means the dishwasher's leak sensor detected moisture where it should not be. It can appear even when you see no leak, for example after the dishwasher was moved. On Waterwall models it can also mean the water reflector is blocked.

Safe checks, in order

  1. Make it safe first. If water is visibly leaking, turn off the power at the circuit breaker and close the water supply valve before you inspect anything. Wipe up the water.
  2. Check the detergent. Use automatic dishwasher detergent only, never hand dish soap, and do not overfill the dispenser. Extra suds can leak and trigger the sensor.
  3. Inspect the door seal. Look for food debris on the rubber gasket around the door, or damage that could let water out.
  4. Check recent changes. If the dishwasher was just moved or installed, water may have splashed near the sensor; give it time to dry. If it was just connected to a new garbage disposal, make sure the disposal's knockout plug was removed.
  5. Check it is level. An unlevel dishwasher can let water pool and reach the sensor. Adjust the legs.

Once everything is dry, restore power and run a short cycle while you watch for drips.

When to call a technician

If there is a real leak from a hose, the tub, or the pump, or if the code persists once everything is dry, contact a qualified installer or Samsung service. Worn door gaskets also need a technician.
