Samsung Washer Sd Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixNo tools neededAlso shown as SUd, 5Ud, 5d, SUdS
Short answer: Sd (or SUd) means your Samsung washer has detected too many suds. Samsung says the washer stops for a short period to let the suds die down and then carries on with the cycle automatically. It is not a breakdown. The cause is almost always too much detergent, or a detergent that is not marked HE (high efficiency).

Fix it yourself: five steps

  1. Let the cycle finish. Do not cancel it. The washer pauses to remove the foam and then resumes on its own. The code may flash again at the end of the cycle as a reminder.
  2. Check the detergent label. Samsung recommends HE (high efficiency) detergent, which is made to produce fewer suds. Regular detergent usually foams much more and is a common cause of this code.
  3. Measure the dose. Use the amount printed on the detergent pack for your load size, not a full cap by habit. For concentrated detergents (labelled 2x, 3x and similar), use less, as the pack directs.
  4. Check the detergent drawer. Make sure detergent is going in the right compartment and that nobody else in the home is adding extra detergent or pods on top of an auto dose setting.
  5. Run an empty test. If suds keep appearing, run a short cycle with an empty drum and no detergent or additives. Samsung notes that if suds still form in an empty cycle, too much detergent or a non-HE detergent has been used before, and the residue is still in the machine. Repeat until the water runs clear.

After switching to the right detergent and dose, the code should stop appearing on the next few loads.

When to call a technician

If you are using an HE detergent at the correct dose, an empty cycle shows no suds, and Sd still appears on every wash, check your user manual for your model and then contact Samsung support to arrange a service visit. If the washer is under warranty, contact Samsung before calling an independent repairer.
