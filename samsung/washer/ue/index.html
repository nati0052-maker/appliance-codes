Samsung Washer UE Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 10 minutesAlso shown as Ub, Ur, E4
Short answer: UE (also Ub) is not a fault. The washer detected that the laundry is unevenly spread in the drum and stopped the spin to protect itself. Rearranging the load and checking that the washer is level fixes it in most cases. Ur means the washer is retrying to balance on its own.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Pause and redistribute the load. Untangle the laundry and spread it evenly around the drum, then close the door and press Start.
  2. Avoid single heavy items. One bathrobe, a pair of jeans, a pillow, or a blanket on its own is hard to balance. Add a few similar items, or wash bulky items on a cycle meant for them.
  3. Check the washer is level. Place a level on top, or rock the corners. Adjust the feet until the washer sits flat and does not wobble on the floor.
  4. Recalibrate if your model supports it. Many Samsung washers can recalibrate their weight sensors with the drum empty. The button combination differs by model (some show Cb while calibrating), so follow the calibration steps in your owner's manual.

Start a Rinse and Spin cycle to confirm the code is gone.

When to call a technician

If UE appears on almost every load even when the washer is level and the load is balanced, the cause may be worn shock absorbers or a faulty sensor that tracks the drum position. Both need a technician.
