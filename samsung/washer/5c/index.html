Samsung Washer 5C Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 20 minutesAlso shown as 5E, SC, SE
Short answer: 5C (or 5E on older models) means the washer cannot drain water properly. Samsung lists a clogged drain pump filter and a kinked, blocked, or badly installed drain hose as the usual causes. Both are checks you can do yourself with a towel and a shallow tray.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Wait before you touch anything. Samsung disables the buttons for about 3 minutes after the code appears. If a hot cycle was running, let the water cool for about an hour before opening the filter.
  2. Check the drain hose. Pull the washer out and make sure the hose behind it is not kinked, crushed against the wall, or frozen. Samsung notes that a badly installed drain hose is the most common cause of drain codes.
  3. Check how far the hose goes into the drain pipe. The hose end should sit no more than about 15 cm (6 inches) inside the standpipe. Pushed in further, it can cause drain problems.
  4. Clean the drain pump filter (front loaders). Open the small panel at the bottom front. Put a shallow tray and towel down, pull out the small emergency drain hose, and let the water run out. Then unscrew the filter cap and remove lint, coins, hair ties, and other debris.
  5. Check the pump propeller. With the filter out, look into the opening and make sure nothing is stopping the small propeller behind it from turning. Refit the filter cap firmly so it does not leak.

Close everything, run a Rinse and Spin cycle, and watch that the water drains. Cleaning the filter every few months prevents this code coming back.

When to call a technician

If the filter and hose are clear and 5C keeps coming back, the drain pump itself may have failed, or there is an electrical fault in the drain circuit. These need a technician. If the washer is under warranty, contact Samsung service first.
