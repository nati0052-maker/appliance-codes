Whirlpool Washer F8 E1 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 15 minutesAlso shown as F08 E01, Lo FL
Short answer: F8 E1 (shown as Lo FL on some models) is a water supply error: the washer is not getting enough water. Whirlpool says both hoses must be attached with water flowing, both taps must be open, and the inlet screens may be clogged.

Fix it yourself: four checks, in order

  1. Let the washer finish draining. After this code the washer drains for about 8 minutes with the door locked. Let it finish, then touch Power once to clear the code.
  2. Open both taps. Whirlpool requires both the hot and cold taps to be on, and both hoses attached with water flowing to the inlet valve.
  3. Straighten the hoses. Remove any kinks in the fill hoses behind the washer.
  4. Clean the inlet screens. Turn the taps off, unscrew the hoses at the back of the washer, and rinse out the small screens in the inlet valve. Reconnect and turn the taps on.

Touch Power again and restart the washer.

When to call a technician

If the code appears again with good water flow and clean screens, Whirlpool says to call for service. The likely fault is the water inlet valve.
