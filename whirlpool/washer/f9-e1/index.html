Whirlpool Washer F9 E1 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 20 to 30 minutesAlso shown as F09 E01, drn
Short answer: F9 E1 means the washer is taking longer than expected to drain, typically over about 8 minutes. Whirlpool's main suspects are a drain hose pushed too far into the drain pipe, a kinked or clogged hose, a clogged drain pump filter, and too many suds.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Check how far the hose goes into the drain pipe. Whirlpool says the hose should go no more than 4.5 inches (about 11 cm) into the standpipe or laundry tub. Deeper than that, water can siphon back into the washer. Use the U-shaped hose form that came with the washer.
  2. Do not seal the drain. Do not tape the hose into the drain pipe or tape over the opening. The drain needs air.
  3. Straighten and clear the hose. Look for kinks behind the washer and blockages in the drain pipe or utility sink.
  4. Clean the drain pump filter (front loaders). Drain the water into a shallow tray, then remove debris from the pump filter. On front loaders also check that the small drain holes in the door seal are clear.
  5. Cut the suds. Use only HE detergent in the measured amount. Too many suds slow or stop draining. Run Rinse and Spin without detergent to clear them.

Run a Drain and Spin cycle to confirm the code is gone.

When to call a technician

If the hose, drain, and filter are all clear, the drain pump or the water level sensing may have failed. Whirlpool recommends scheduling service.
