Whirlpool Dryer PF Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 2 minutesMeans power failure
Short answer: PF means a power failure interrupted the drying cycle. The control board lost power, or detected electrical noise that it read as the wrong voltage, and stopped the cycle. It is a notice, not a broken part. In most cases you clear it and restart the load.

Fix it yourself: four steps, in order

  1. Clear the code. Press and hold START to restart the cycle where it left off, or press POWER to clear the display and start over.
  2. Give the dryer a moment after power returns. The control needs about 10 seconds to power up. Whirlpool notes that pressing buttons before that delay is over can also bring up PF, so wait a few seconds before touching the panel.
  3. Reset the breaker after an outage. Switch off the dryer's circuit breaker at the panel, wait one minute, and switch it back on. Then watch the dryer for one minute to confirm PF does not come back.
  4. Run a short cycle. Start a normal cycle and check that the dryer runs, heats, and finishes without the code returning.

An electric dryer is usually wired to a double breaker, and some panels use two fuses. If only half of it has tripped, the dryer may light up but act oddly, so make sure the whole breaker is reset.

When to call a technician

If PF keeps showing up when there has been no outage, or it returns right after a breaker reset, the dryer or the home's wiring may have a power supply problem. Whirlpool recommends contacting them or scheduling service if the steps above do not clear it. If other appliances or lights in the house also flicker, have an electrician check the supply.
