Frigidaire Dishwasher i20 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 20 minutes
Short answer: i20 means the dishwasher is not draining properly. Start with the filter on the tub floor, then follow the drain hose from the dishwasher to the sink. A blocked, pinched, or low drain hose, or a new garbage disposal with its knockout plug still in, explains most cases.

Fix it yourself: five checks, in order

  1. Clean the filter. Remove the lower rack and lift out the filter assembly (Frigidaire calls part of it the glass trap). Rinse away food soil under warm water and refit it firmly.
  2. Check the drain hose for kinks. Look under the sink for a hose that is pinched, crushed by stored items, or bent sharply.
  3. Check the hose for a blockage. If the hose is easy to disconnect at the sink end, place a towel and bowl underneath, take it off, and check it is clear. Reconnect it securely.
  4. Check the hose height. Frigidaire says the drain hose must reach a minimum of 32 inches from the floor. If it runs low along the cabinet floor, raise it and secure it near the underside of the counter.
  5. New disposal? Check the knockout plug. A new garbage disposal comes with a plastic plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. If it was never knocked out, the dishwasher cannot drain.

To clear the code, press or hold Cancel. If it stays, switch off the breaker or unplug the dishwasher for 3 minutes, then run a short cycle.

When to call a technician

If the filter is clean, the hose is clear and at the right height, the disposal is open, and i20 still returns, the fault is likely inside the dishwasher, such as the drain pump. That needs the lower panels removed and the power and water isolated. If the dishwasher is under warranty, contact Frigidaire service before opening it.
