Bosch Dishwasher E15 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually needs a technicianAbout 10 minutes of safe checksLeak protection active
Short answer: E15 means a safety switch found water in the base of the dishwasher, so the leak protection system has switched on. Bosch's own advice is short: turn off the water supply to the dishwasher and contact Bosch Customer Support. The water in the base came from somewhere inside the machine, and finding that source is a service job.

Safe steps while you wait for service

  1. Turn off the water supply. This is the step Bosch asks for. The shutoff valve is usually under the kitchen sink on the line that runs to the dishwasher.
  2. Cut the power. Switch the dishwasher off, then unplug it or turn off its circuit breaker before you touch anything near the base.
  3. Wipe up any water you can see. Check the floor in front of and beside the dishwasher and dry it so nobody slips and the floor is not damaged.
  4. Note what you saw. Write down your model number (usually on a label at the edge of the door), when E15 appeared, and whether you saw water on the floor or lots of suds in the tub. This helps the technician.
  5. Contact Bosch. In the US, Bosch lists Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904, plus a contact form, online chat and a service booking page on bosch-home.com.

Do not keep restarting cycles to clear the code. If the machine is leaking inside, each cycle adds more water to the base.

When to call a technician

Right away. Bosch treats every E15 as a reason to shut off the water and call support. Water in the base means something inside is leaking, such as a hose, seal or pump, and reaching it means removing panels. Leave that to Bosch service or a qualified appliance technician.
