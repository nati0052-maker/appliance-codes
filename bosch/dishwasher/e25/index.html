Bosch Dishwasher E25 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Some fixes need toolsAbout 20 to 40 minutesDrain pump blocked
Short answer: E25 means the drain pump in your Bosch dishwasher is blocked, or the pump cover is loose, missing or not clipped in properly. The fix is usually to unplug the dishwasher, take out the filter, lift off the small pump cover underneath and clear out whatever is stuck. Bosch suggests a spoon to pry the cover loose. Watch out for broken glass in the pump.

How to fix it, step by step

  1. Switch off and unplug. Turn the dishwasher off and pull the plug or switch off its circuit breaker. Bosch warns the pump may be blocked with glass fragments, so you could cut yourself. Gloves help.
  2. Take out the lower rack and filter. Slide out the lower rack, sponge out any standing water, then twist and lift the filter out of the floor of the tub. Rinse it while it is out.
  3. Find the pump cover. With the filter removed you will see a small cover in the bottom of the sump. If you cannot find it, your owner's manual shows where it is on your model.
  4. Remove the cover. Bosch suggests using a spoon to pry the cover up until it clicks. Then grip it, pull upward until you feel resistance, and pull it forward to take it out.
  5. Clear the pump. Remove any debris you find, such as glass, bones, seeds or labels, and sponge out the water. Go slowly, since some pieces may be sharp.
  6. Check the impeller. Bosch says to check that the pump impeller, the small paddle wheel under the cover, can turn freely. If it is stuck or broken after clearing it, that is a job for a technician.
  7. Refit everything firmly. Put the pump cover back so it locks in place. A loose or missing cover alone can trigger E25. Refit the filter until it is secure, slide the rack back in, restore power and run a short program to test.

When to call a technician

Call for help if E25 comes back after you have cleared the pump and refitted the cover firmly, if the impeller will not turn, or if the cover is cracked or missing. In the US, Bosch lists Customer Support at 1-800-944-2904, plus a contact form and online chat on bosch-home.com. If you see water on the floor, stop using the dishwasher and get help, as that is a different problem.
