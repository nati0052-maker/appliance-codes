Bosch Dishwasher E24 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 15 to 30 minutesDrain problem
Short answer: E24 means your Bosch dishwasher is not draining properly. You may see water sitting in the bottom of the tub, a cycle that pauses or stops, or hear gurgling or humming. The usual causes are a clogged filter, a kinked or blocked hose, or a new garbage disposal that still has its knockout plug in place. Most of these you can check yourself with no tools.

How to fix it, step by step

  1. Switch off and unplug. Turn the dishwasher off and unplug it or switch off its circuit breaker. If a cycle just ran, give the water a few minutes to cool.
  2. Clean the filter. Pull out the lower rack, scoop or sponge out any standing water, then twist out the filter in the floor of the tub. Rinse it under the tap and scrub off grease and food bits with a soft brush. Bosch lists a filter that needs cleaning as a cause of E24.
  3. Check the pump cover. With the filter out, look at the cover underneath it. Bosch says to make sure it is not loose. Clear out any debris you can reach, make sure the cover sits firmly, then refit the filter until it locks.
  4. Look for kinked hoses. Open the cabinet under the sink and follow the hoses from the dishwasher. Bosch says to make sure the hose is not kinked or damaged. Straighten any sharp bends and move stored items that press on it.
  5. Check the sink connection. Bosch asks you to check that the siphon connection under the sink is sealed. Look where the dishwasher hose joins the sink drain or disposal and make sure it is secure.
  6. New garbage disposal? Check the knockout plug. Many new disposals ship with a plug sealing the dishwasher inlet. If yours was installed recently and E24 started soon after, the plug may still be in. Bosch lists this as a cause. With the disposal unplugged, the plug is knocked out from inside the inlet; if you are unsure how, ask whoever installed it.
  7. Reset and test. Restore power and reset the dishwasher the way your owner's manual describes. Run a short program and check that the tub is empty at the end.

When to call a technician

If E24 keeps coming back after a clean filter, straight hoses and a sealed connection, the blockage or fault is probably deeper in the drain hose or pump.
