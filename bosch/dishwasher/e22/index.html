Bosch Dishwasher E22 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually an easy fixAbout 10 to 15 minutesFilter blocked
Short answer: E22 means the filter in the bottom of your Bosch dishwasher is blocked. Bosch describes it as a maintenance code: food scraps, seeds, labels or bits of glass have built up in the filter and are restricting the water. Cleaning the filter by hand fixes it in most cases, and you do not need any tools.

Fix it yourself: five steps, in order

  1. Turn the dishwasher off. Cancel the cycle and switch it off before reaching into the tub. If there is hot water in the bottom, let it cool first.
  2. Pull out the lower rack. This gives you a clear view of the filter in the floor of the tub.
  3. Remove the filter. Lift it out of the floor of the tub. Bosch filters are designed to come out by hand; your owner's manual shows the exact way your model releases.
  4. Clean it properly. Rinse the filter under running water, then use a little mild soap and a soft brush to clear grease and stuck particles from the mesh. Do not use wire brushes or scourers, which can damage the mesh.
  5. Check the opening, then refit. Look into the hole the filter came from and pick out any large debris by hand, taking care with broken glass. Put the filter back so it sits flat and locks in place. A loose filter lets debris past and can cause other faults.

Close the door, switch the dishwasher back on, and run a short cycle. To stop E22 returning, scrape large food scraps off plates before loading and clean the filter regularly, as your owner's manual recommends.

When to call a technician

If the filter is clean, seated firmly, and E22 still appears, or the dishwasher also starts showing drain codes such as E24 or E25, there may be a blockage further down in the sump or pump. Bosch customer service can help, and in the US Bosch lists 1-800-944-2904. If the dishwasher is under warranty, contact Bosch before taking anything apart.
