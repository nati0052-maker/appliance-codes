Bosch Dishwasher E09 Error Code: What It Means and How to Fix It

Usually needs a technicianAbout 5 minutes to try a resetHeater fault
Short answer: E09 means the dishwasher has found a fault with the heater built into its heat pump, the part that warms the wash water. Bosch's advice is to reset the power at the breaker for 30 seconds. If the code is still there after that, call Bosch Customer Support. There are no user checks beyond the reset.

Safe steps: try one reset

  1. Switch the dishwasher off. Press the power button and let the machine go quiet.
  2. Reset the breaker for 30 seconds. Turn off the dishwasher's circuit breaker in your electrical panel, wait 30 seconds, then turn it back on. This is the reset Bosch gives for E09.
  3. Run a cycle and watch the display. If E09 does not come back, the fault may have been a one-off. If it returns, stop and do not keep restarting cycles.
  4. Turn off the water if the code stays. Bosch's guidance on some sites is to close the water tap and switch off the appliance when E09 appears. The shutoff is usually under the kitchen sink.
  5. Note your model number. It is on a label on the edge of the door. Write it down with when E09 appeared, so the service call goes faster.

E09 is an electrical fault. Do not open panels or try to test the heater yourself.

When to call a technician

If E09 is still there after the breaker reset. Bosch says to call Bosch Customer Support, listed in the US at 1-800-944-2904. Bosch's support page for some countries goes further and says not to try fixing E09 at home for safety reasons. The heater sits inside the machine and needs the power and water isolated to reach, so leave it to Bosch service or a qualified appliance technician.
